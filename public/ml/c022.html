P only had small 2-minute deviations. Q contained a night 14 minutes below the mean. For the getaway, Q is dangerous and P is not. We need a measure of spread that gives a lot of weight to large deviations. A large downward deviation is a camera that comes back on too early. If the measure drowns it among small deviations, the driver will believe the outages are more regular than they are. That is the worst mistake he can make.
